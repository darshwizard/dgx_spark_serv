Firmware/community findings (not locally reproduced)
Forum382536 and386805 report EC3.13.7 accepting ACPI fan writes without RPM change.
An Oct28 correction reports EC-only downgrade3.5.8 with currentSoC2.158.15 permits
ACPI targets, without a kernel module. The previous broad incompatibility claim
was retracted: one mailbox path failed, but ACPI had not been tested.
Community driver floors preserve the controller's ability to add cooling; the
reported ACPI target replaces automatic control at ordinary temperatures, and
low targets reportedly stall fans. This is not an endorsed procedure for our host.
The exact firmware mechanism and proposed dual curves remain community claims.
Our observed RPM steps resemble the reported slow curve; boot selection is unproven.
Owner preference: keep firmware and EC settings unchanged.
